I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) at University College London. I am also an Affiliate of CEPR and CESifo, an Associate Editor of the Journal of the European Economic Association, and an Advisory Editor of Games and Economic Behavior. I received my PhD from Harvard University and completed my postdoc at Princeton University.

My [research](/research/) is in behavioral economics, experimental economics, and political economy. I am particularly interested in belief updating, communication, and political polarization.

See my [CV](/cv/) and [Google Scholar]({{ site.author.googlescholar }}) for more information.

You can contact me at [michael.thaler@ucl.ac.uk](mailto:michael.thaler@ucl.ac.uk).
