VP Decks

A destination's first icon selects a deck. Drawn icons score M² + W² + H² + B² + max² + min².

Legend

  • Destination Explore to see its first icon; exploit to draw from that icon's VP deck.
  • Airplane Exploit for an optional free flight to an unlocked hex up to 5 hexes away.
  • Tea Bank 2 extra energy for your next turn.
  • Spy Reveal all adjacent hidden hexes without exploring them.
  • Study Unlock the next ring for that player.
  • Endgame The first revealed Cat starts the final-turn countdown; both exploited Cats end it immediately.

    Face-down Unknown hexes stay private until exploited or known by both players.
